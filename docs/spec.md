# matops — MVP Product Spec (v1)

**First academy release, ADR 135**

Status: implementation is in final static verification. Browser, device, privacy, and restore checks remain open.

- **FA1: Adult person records.** Staff can create a student without a login. One verified account may link to the person for self-service. There is no guardian access.
- **FA2: Staff-assigned memberships.** An approved branch student needs a staff-assigned shared plan to train. There is no student plan shop or purchase record.
- **FA3: Monthly evidence.** A month is unpaid, reported, confirmed, or rejected. Reports and staff decisions preserve the actor, time, version, amount, currency, deadline, and time zone evidence.
- **FA4: Access deadline.** A new booking or self check-in stops after the fixed seven-day local grace unless a claim is pending. Existing reservations stay. Older unpaid months remain visible but do not create a lasting hold.
- **FA5: Rejection hold.** A rejected month creates a person hold for the assignment's branch coverage. Month rollover and plan changes cannot bypass it. An owner or manager must resolve it explicitly.
- **FA6: One booking window.** Each branch accepts 1-90 local calendar days and defaults to 7. Today is the first date. Lists, direct links, self check-in, and hosted visitors use the same branch-local rule.
- **FA7: Safe schedule maintenance.** Owners extend the generated calendar manually. Rebuild starts from a versioned preview, preserves past and one-off classes, never revives cancelled classes, and refuses booked stale classes.
- **FA8: Request-driven notice delivery.** A class or visitor action saves the notice and recipient snapshots in its transaction. The request sends a bounded first batch. Staff can send remaining recipients or retry failed recipients. Accepted recipients cannot be resent.
- **FA9: Hosted visitors.** `/book/<academy-code>` lists allowed classes, books within the branch rule, and returns private status and cancellation tokens. MatOps has no external booking API, public API keys, or origin settings.
- **FA10: Focused records.** Coach counts use ended, non-cancelled classes with a current attendance review. Drifting shows the full operational list. Referrals show confirmed membership-month evidence without bonuses. Rank corrections append history.
- **FA11: Optional and removed modules.** Curriculum and referrals remain optional. Shopping, announcements, broad Insights, student training statistics, scheduled jobs, and job health panels are outside this release.
- **FA12: Fresh install.** The schema ships as one fresh baseline. There is no legacy conversion, backfill, or compatibility rollout.

These rules replace the ADR 134 release block below. See [ADR 135](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/135-first-academy-fresh-install-contract.md).

**Superseded ADR 134 release rules**

- **FA1: Students exist without accounts.** A gym can create a person with a name and optional contact details, enroll them at a branch, assign a membership, and record attendance. Linking a verified login is optional. A guardian may book and cancel for a linked child, but cannot use staff actions or manage the child's private record.
- **FA2: One payment record.** Shared memberships are the only plan and paid-month system. Branch payment enforcement remains optional. Existing bookings stay booked when access changes. MatOps records a paid flag and never describes it as bank revenue.
- **FA3: One navigation owner.** Organization and branch pages use one persistent shell. Organization pages keep the selected branch in the URL. Organization and selected-branch badges remain visible together and retain their separate permission scopes.
- **FA4: Historical attendance is accountable.** Owners and managers may correct or finish attendance older than 14 days with a reason. Instructors keep the 14-day window. Historical changes record the actor, time, previous value, new value, and reason.
- **FA5: Class email survives request failure.** Cancelling or changing a class queues member and visitor email in the same transaction. Delivery retries later with a stable key. Inbox and native push remain available for linked accounts.
- **FA6: Visitor bookings return.** The public booking result keeps its private token. The return page shows changed or cancelled class details and accepts a safe cancellation until the token expires.
- **FA7: Scheduled work is visible.** Email delivery runs every five minutes. Membership reminders, overdue cancellation, and calendar extension run daily. Owners and managers see late jobs and exhausted email work for their own gym.
- **FA8: Import is reviewed before it writes.** Owners and managers can paste or upload up to 500 CSV rows. The preview validates branch, membership, dates, contact fields, and possible duplicates. Commit repeats the preview and writes every included row in one transaction.
- **FA9: Export is independent of screen filters.** Organization exports include archived people, enrollments, guardians, memberships, payment records, attendance, and attendance corrections. Each file uses bounded pages.
- **FA10: Optional modules start off.** Curriculum and referrals remain hidden and refuse direct API use until an owner or manager turns them on for the organization.

These rules replace older account-only student, branch dues, split shell, best-effort email, and detached scheduler rules. See [ADR 134](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/134-first-academy-release.md).

**Attendance review, ADR 133:** implementation in progress. O1 and O5 below describe the approved target. Migration, static checks and browser acceptance remain unverified.

**Staff and student separation (ADR 132)**

- **ST1:** Members lists students, including pending student requests. Staff lists owners, managers, instructors, and pending coach requests. Inactive staff remain in Staff.
- **ST2:** Active staff train at their branch without a paid membership. Staff role, approval status, and payment standing are separate. Inherited organization access creates no student enrollment.
- **ST3:** Roster warnings, activation previews, booking review, and overdue cancellation respect staff access. A staff role at one branch does not exempt student access elsewhere.
- **ST4:** Staff participants remain in class attendance with role badges and count toward capacity. Teaching alone creates no student attendance.
- **ST5:** Staff lifecycle controls preserve history. Moving an instructor to Members is explicit and creates no paid plan. Branch actions cannot remove inherited organization authority.
- **ST6:** New assignments cannot sell access only to staff branches. Existing assignments and payment history require review, not automatic cancellation. The person profile marks staff-only plans for review.

These rules replace earlier statements that owners and instructors require membership coverage. See [ADR 132](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/132-staff-roster-isolation.md).

**Student membership selection (ADR 130)**

Status: code implemented. Workspace typecheck passes 17/17 and lint passes 15/15. [Decision](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/130-students-choose-a-membership.md). Tests were written but have not run. The migration has not been applied. Browser control timed out, so browser acceptance remains open.

- **SP1: Approval comes before purchase.** A student joins using the existing branch code or invite. While pending, they can browse that branch's schedule and class details. They cannot book or check in. Account edits remain available. Approved students can choose a published shared membership covering their current branch.
- **SP2: Plans explain their coverage.** A student sees the price, currency, calendar month and every included branch before selecting. A BJJ student can choose a BJJ plus Gym bundle when it covers their BJJ branch. Private plans, plans without a usable price, and plans with unavailable coverage cannot be selected. Owners and managers choose which plans to publish; existing plans start private.
- **SP3: Selection reserves no access.** Selection creates a pending purchase, not an active assignment. The student sees bank details and a payment reference, transfers money outside the app, then selects **I paid**. Neither selection nor the claim grants access. A student can cancel an unclaimed purchase. A claimed payment needs staff review. Staff can close rejected requests after recording the outcome. A student can cancel a rejected free request. Pending students may preview the catalog, but cannot select a plan.
- **SP4: Confirmation activates the membership.** An owner or manager checks the transfer and confirms the purchase. Confirmation creates the assignment once and records a paid month only for a paid plan. Free plans require approval, but create no payment record. Later months remain usable while their effective historical price is zero. It adds missing included branches as active student memberships. It preserves existing roles and never restores a suspended, rejected, archived or removed branch membership. A changed restriction requires staff review before activation.
- **SP5: Calendar months stay explicit.** Students choose this month or next month. The full monthly amount and covered dates appear before payment; there is no proration. A future membership does not permit booking before its start. A stale price, changed coverage or a month that has passed sends a confirmed transfer to a staff resolution queue. It grants no access until staff handle the issue; no automatic refund occurs.
- **SP6: Existing access rules remain separate.** First purchases receive no access before confirmation. Existing renewal grace and staff privileges remain. Users may cancel their own existing bookings while payment blocks new bookings. Older provisional booking records remain readable, but new pending bookings are refused in both clients by the shared API.
- **SP7: The first release is web selection.** Student purchase and staff review use the web app. Existing mobile requests retain their shapes and receive the same approval checks. Organization invitations, branch discovery outside the relevant catalog, trial policies, card processing and student plan replacement are outside this release.

These rules replace earlier permissions for pending members to book or check in. Historical release notes below describe their original releases.

**The manager runs every branch, and a plan change waits for the 1st (v7.7)**

Status: **built, not clicked through**. Decisions: [ADR 129](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/129-the-manager-runs-every-branch.md). Run file: [PLAN.md](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/PLAN.md).

- **MB1: A manager runs every branch.** An active manager of the gym enters every live branch without a membership there, and is written down as a manager, never as an owner. They run the schedule, attendance, local members and their approvals, instructor assignments, leads, plans, dues, claims and ordinary branch settings. Four actions stay with owners: making or removing an owner, permanent erase, deleting a branch, and the commercial account. Management access enrols nobody as a student: a manager who does not train at a branch cannot book a class there. Removing their gym membership takes effect on the next request.
- **MB2: Adding a branch to a plan enrols the people on it.** One save does the whole job: it changes the coverage and gives the plan's live holders a membership at the branch it just added. A branch place that is paused, archived or removed is never touched, and comes back as a decision for a person to make. `Re-check coverage` becomes a retry that shows only while somebody is still missing, and it stays safe to press twice. A save computed against a coverage somebody else has changed is refused with a fresh preview instead of being applied to a stale list.
- **MB3: Changing a plan starts on the 1st.** The month they are in does not move: the old plan runs to the end of it with its amount, its paid tick and its claim intact, and the new one starts on the first day of the next month in the gym's own membership time zone. Nothing is prorated and nothing is refunded, and the new plan starts with no payment record of its own. Staff can take the change back until it starts; after that they schedule a new one. Two submits make one change. Coming back to a plan somebody held before keeps the earlier period and its receipts.
- **MB4: Losing coverage cancels nothing.** Ending a membership, or dropping a branch from a plan, leaves every booking where it is: the reservation, the place it holds and the attendance history all stay, and nobody is told their class was cancelled. The affected sessions become a review list of future bookings at a branch that will not admit the person when the session starts. It clears itself when coverage comes back, when staff cancel the booking from the class, or when the session passes. An unpaid month is not on that list: that is the dues gate, and the overdue sweep keeps it.
- **MB5: Gym and branch work sit in one menu.** Standing at a branch, an owner or manager sees `Gym management` beside the branch's own screens, so shared payments are one click away. Settings are named `Gym settings` and `Branch settings`. The branch picker's way out is `Gym overview`, which is a place to go and not a filter. A gym with one branch shows its name and no picker. Switching branches keeps the screen and the date where the destination has them, and drops the person, class and session ids of the branch being left.
- **MB6: People and staff are one screen.** Staff is a tab inside People, over the same rows, and `/staff` redirects onto it. A person's own page carries their branches, what covers them at each, their branch assignments and their per-branch approvals, and a branch badge opens that person at that branch. An approval is still a per-branch decision. Ordinary roles only: nothing here makes an owner or a manager.
- **MB7: The overview answers what needs doing.** `Needs attention` sits above the figures: payment claims, people waiting to join, coaches waiting on a seat, members nothing covers, and bookings that need a review. Every line opens the person, the queue or the booking it is about. Setup steps link to the branch that is actually behind. A branch is only short of a coach when a mat has nobody running it; a solo owner who teaches counts, and a general space runs sessions without one.
- **MB8: The branch picker is beside the schedule.** On the phone the branch name in the header opens the picker, so students compare two schedules without opening Profile. A member of one branch is offered nothing. A switch is committed only after the session change it needs succeeds; a failure leaves them where they were and says so.

**Not in this release:** a level under the branch, rooms or resources, a new branch type, an all-branches student calendar, immediate plan replacement, proration, refunds, credit transfer, automatic cancellation when coverage ends, and staff invitations sent by email.

**Finishing the organization pivot (v7.6)**

Status: **built, not clicked through**. Decisions: [ADR 128](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/docs/issues/128-registration-creates-the-organization-only.md). Run file: [PLAN.md](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/PLAN.md). Loop file: [EPIC.md](/Users/kuna/projects/matops/EPIC.md).

- **FP1: Registration creates the gym, not a mat.** Starting a gym writes the organization and makes you its owner. It writes no branch: no place, no join code, no schedule, no curriculum track. The owner lands on `/overview`.
- **FP2: A gym with no branches is a normal state.** It is where a new owner begins, not a broken setup. The zero-branch overview offers one action, **Create your first branch**. Overview, Settings and Profile work there, and every organization reading answers zero rather than failing. Nothing repairs the gym behind the owner's back. Join by code stays for the person it was written for: signed in, owning no gym, belonging to no branch.
- **FP3: The owner picks the first branch.** They choose its type and its name. There is no default type, so a gym whose first branch is a general space is as ordinary as one that starts with a mat. The first live branch becomes the default branch, and it takes the gym's own id where that does not conflict, so a join code, a QR poster or a check-in link printed against the gym still resolves.
- **FP4: Creating a branch is safe to repeat.** Two requests at once produce one first branch and one default, never two. A half-finished attempt is repaired into the same branch. Every branch gets the creator as its owner; only a BJJ academy also gets belts, curriculum and the visitor poster. The create form is told what the gym may open, so it never offers a branch type the server will refuse.
- **FP5: A new branch is on shared memberships from its first day.** Every branch created after this release runs the membership gate, a BJJ academy included. Legacy branch dues stay only on branches that existed before the cutover and have not moved, they move through one guided flow instead of a switch that lives in Settings forever, and once a branch has moved its old plans and dues are read only.
- **FP6: A membership plan carries a price.** A plan has a monthly amount and a currency. A price change names the month it starts, so it reaches existing members from that month and never rewrites a month already behind them. There is no proration in this release. A member sees the amount, the currency, where to pay, their reference and the deadline on their standing, their claim and their history; the owner sees the same amount on plan cards, on the assignment dialog and on the month board. A month marked paid is somebody saying money was sent, and the gym still counts revenue only where the owner confirmed it.
- **FP7: A manager runs the gym's daily work.** The gym can have managers beside its owners. A manager reads the gym's people, plans, payments, staff and reports, assigns memberships, confirms claims, posts announcements and finishes setup. Deleting the gym, making or removing an owner, the commercial account and switching a branch onto shared memberships stay with owners. Every one of those rules is enforced where the work happens, so hiding a menu item is decoration and never the check.
- **FP8: Setup is guided, not guessed.** The gym carries a setup list the server works out from what exists: the gym's name, the first branch, its place and time zone, a schedule, staff, bank details, a membership plan, members assigned to it, readiness and the first invite. Each item opens the screen that finishes it. The list stays on the overview as a compact progress card until it is done. A branch's own list speaks its type: a mat says classes, curriculum and visitor links, a general space says sessions. The overview also says which branches are not ready to open, naming the reason: no schedule, no staff, nobody covered, no public classes, membership setup unfinished.
- **FP9: One set of words, used everywhere.** A **gym** is the business somebody owns. A **branch** is any one place it runs. A BJJ branch is an **academy**, or a **mat** when the sentence is about training on it. A non-BJJ branch is a **space**. A **session** is one dated thing on a schedule, and at an academy that session is a **class**. Nothing is called an organization or an entity in front of a person: those are the words in the database, not the words in a gym.

- **FP10: What a gym pays is written down, never charged.** The signed-out page names two offers. **Academy** is one BJJ academy with everything in it, free for pilot academies until a date agreed with them and expected to cost about EUR 49 a month after that. **Facility** is a mat plus another bookable space sharing one set of people and one membership, and it is described as **not on sale** until that part works. A gym reads its own account in gym Settings: the offer, the status, the date free use ends (marked when it has passed), the agreed price and the billing contact. There is no card on file and no checkout anywhere in the product, so every change to any of this happens by email.

**Not in this release:** a third branch type, automatic payment collection or any payment provider, proration, waitlists, private hire, room or equipment inventory, a shared curriculum library, referrals across branches, public visitor booking at a general space, logo storage, moving a member or a class between branches, and deleting the legacy dues tables.

- **OD6: Staff across branches.** One row per person, with a role badge per branch and a marker for a pending coach request. Approving, promoting and setting a role stay on that branch's Coaches page.

As-built notes on the seven rules above. OD1: a route group, so no URL changed, and the frame is `shell-frame.tsx`, shared with the branch shell rather than copied. OD2: the account lives on `membership_policy.bank_details`, and the reference is `paymentReference(name, assignmentId)`, derived at read time and never stored, the same function ADR 40 uses. `setPolicy` writes grace, the zone and the account together, so the Settings screen carries one form with one Save; two forms would blank each other's field. OD3: the server picks the month from `monthOf(now)` in the gym's own zone, so a claim names no month; a rejection writes no audit row, because `membership_month_audit` is the history of the paid flag; `markMonth` clears the claim in both directions. OD4: the preview is `loadRosterStanding` filtered to `covered === false`, which is the same pure rule the gate runs, and it counts every active member whatever their role, because booking gives staff no bypass. OD5: the dues reminder skips an activated branch from inside the CLAIM subselect in `packages/db/src/dues-reminder.ts`, not the resolve, so a branch switched back is not left silently unstamped. OD6: `staffRows` filters the member directory rather than reading again, so the two screens cannot disagree, and the badge counts a waiting coach seat per branch. OD7: `announcementFanOut` writes one inbox row per person per branch and pushes once per person, which is why it does not go through `deliver`.

**Not in this release:** logo upload, deleting an organization, any money movement, referrals across branches, a leads roll-up, a shared curriculum library, an organization-level invite list, and moving a member or a class between branches. The curriculum stays per branch.

## 1. What v1 is

A grappling academy's **schedule + attendance**, done flawlessly, for two audiences:

- **Students** book classes from their phone.
- **Owners/instructors** run the schedule, member list, and attendance from the web.

Nothing else. The bet (crowded niche) is that _simple + fast_ beats _feature-rich_. Every screen below earns its place in one of the two core loops.

### The two core loops

- **Student loop — "train":** open app → see this week's classes → **book my next class in ≤3 taps** → show up. That speed is the product.
- **Owner loop — "run the week":** set the recurring schedule once → approve new members → see who's coming → tap through attendance after class.

### The student-friction principle (the make-or-break)

The owner is the buyer, but the **student is the user**. The owner's data only exists if students actually use the app. So v1 is built to ask students for **almost nothing**:

- **Let them in first, confirm them later.** Anyone (student or coach) can sign up, see the schedule, and book **before** the owner approves them. Until approval they are **view-only** — they can browse, book, and check in, but see no member names and get no tools. Approval assigns the real role.
- **Booking is never blocked; capacity is a guide.** Anyone can book any class. When a class passes its capacity it shows in **red** ("22/20 — may be busy") and asks you to confirm — the owner manages the room. _(Hard caps, priority for members, waitlists, no-show and cancel cut-offs are the next iteration.)_ _(Amended by ADR 82: an owner can switch on the **membership gate** (§4), which locks booking for an approved student with no plan or a long-unpaid month, after a grace window with a visible countdown. **Off by default**; capacity itself still never blocks, and the gym QR check-in always works.)_
- **Showing up is one tap.** A **QR code inside the gym** marks attendance (and books first if needed). Booking is optional intent; the QR is the real "I'm here".
- **No app? No problem.** The same gym QR opens a **short web form** for **first-time visitors** — captured as a lead the owner can follow up (and send an app link to). It's a first touch, not a repeat path.

### Roles

| Role                           | Client       | Can do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**                      | Web          | Everything: academy settings, invites, member list + **approve members and coaches**, all templates/classes, attendance                                                                                                                                        |
| **Instructor**                 | Web          | _(after owner approval)_ Run the **whole schedule**, **take attendance**, and see **their own Coaches card**; _not_ the Members screen, the Referrals ledger, Payments controls, academy settings, invites, or approvals                                       |
| **Manager (gym)** _(v7.6)_     | Web          | _(organization level, ADR 128)_ The gym's people, plans, payments, staff and reports; assign memberships, confirm claims, announce, finish setup; _not_ deleting the gym, owner changes, the commercial account, or switching a branch onto shared memberships |
| **Pending (student or coach)** | Mobile / Web | **View-only:** browse the schedule, book/cancel own, check in. **No** member names, no tools — until the owner approves                                                                                                                                        |
| **Student**                    | Mobile       | _(after approval)_ View schedule, **book/cancel**, see who's in a class, own classes/profile                                                                                                                                                                   |

_(Everyone joins **pending/view-only**; the invite link carries the intended role; **owner approval** grants it — this is uniform for students and coaches, so a forwarded coach link can't hand out real access. Roles are **per membership**: one login can be an owner at one gym and a student at another. The **client is the lens** — web shows gyms you run, mobile shows gyms you train at. Multi-academy is native; v1 shows one active academy with a **switcher** if you belong to more than one.)_

## 2. Student mobile app (Expo)

### S1 · Sign in / Sign up _(as light as possible)_

- **Social login** or **email + magic link** (passwordless — no password to invent). One tap where possible. Built: **Continue with Google** on **web** alongside the magic link (ADR 35); Google is shown only when the server has OAuth creds set. **Mobile is magic-link only for now** — the Google button is deferred (the iOS browser→app session return doesn't land; ADR 35, `docs/mobile-google-login-handoff.md`).
- **Fields:** first name, second name, phone, **email (required)**. Email is the sign-in identity (the magic link goes there), so it's not optional for members. Nothing else.
- Reached before joining (S2). On mobile v1 the student picks the academy by its ID; sign-in comes first, then the join.

### S2 · Join an academy _(the onboarding spine)_

- **Mobile v1 (docs/issues/32):** the owner shares the academy's **ID — a plain 6-digit number**. The student **types the ID** in the app → we find the academy → _"Join **{Academy}**"_ → confirm + minimal profile → a **pending** membership. No link, no deep link, no list to scroll.
  - The confirm step shows the gym's **address** when it has one _(v2.9, ADR 68)_, so a mistyped digit that finds a real gym is caught before joining.
  - Edge: no academy for that ID → clear "No gym found with that ID — check the number with your gym."
- **Web + a later mobile iteration:** opens via an **invite link** (deep link `matops://join/<code>` or an https universal link) — the academy is preselected. On mobile this returns after v1 (docs/issues/31); the invite links stay live for the web (per-role, incl. instructors).
- **Every form asks for a person the same way** _(v6.5, ADR 119)_. A **phone** is a country picker beside the number, and the picker opens on the academy's own **country** (O6). Until an owner sets one it is guessed from the time zone, which is only a guess: zones cross borders. What is stored carries the country code (`+351912345678`), so one person's number is one string however they wrote it. An **email** completes the domain once you type the `@`. Both are one component each, so the join forms, the profile, the walk-in row, the wall QR, the public booking page and sign-in cannot drift on what they accept. The **mobile app keeps its own fields for now**, so a number typed there may still arrive without a country code.
- **Web join is one form** _(v2.2, ADR 57; amended by ADR 59):_ a signed-out visitor on an invite link fills **first name, last name, phone and email together**, then gets the magic link. Opening that link returns them to the same form with **every field already filled in**, and one press of **Join** finishes it. The profile travels in the link, so it survives the mail app opening a different browser. The link **prefills, it never joins on its own**: an invite URL is public and hand-craftable, so a link that both joined and wrote the profile could be sent to any signed-in person to rewrite their name and phone (ADR 59). A **Google** sign-in skips the email step and is asked for name and phone on return.
- **No waiting wall.** The student lands straight on the schedule (S4) and can **book right away** (provisionally). Approval happens in the background.
- _(Joining a **second** academy later: enter another gym's ID while signed in → another pending membership → appears in the S7 switcher.)_

### S3 · Pending banner _(not a blocker)_

No blank waiting screen. A **slim banner** on the schedule: "Pending approval — you can browse and book; your gym will confirm you soon." Bookings made now are **provisional** (§4). When the owner approves, the banner clears and a **push** ("You're in at {Academy}") lands.

**Archived member (§4, v4.1, ADR 84):** an archived member has **no** enrolled access, the same as a suspended one, and sees the "your access to this academy has been removed" message. The row and every record attached to it are kept, so **Restore** (O4) brings the whole person back. `archived` is a distinct status from `removed`: `removed` marks an applicant an owner **declined**, who was never a member, and who therefore never shows on the Archived tab.

**Suspended member (§4):** a suspended member has **no** enrolled access — the server rejects the schedule / dues / stats reads. Instead of a scary "couldn't load" error, the student sees a **calm notice** ("Your membership is paused — contact your gym to restore your access") on Schedule and My classes; Profile stays reachable (edit details, switch academy, sign out) with the same notice in place of the rank + dues sections. An owner reverses this with **Restore** (O4).

### S4 · Schedule _(home — the core screen)_

**On the web too** _(v2.2, ADR 57; brought to parity in v3.2, ADR 73):_ a student signed in on the web gets this same week — grouped by day, Today first, with **Book** and **Cancel** on the row and the same "looks full, book anyway?" confirm. It reads `class.mine` and the `booking` router, the same calls the app makes, so the two clients cannot drift apart. Only the in-app QR scan (S8) and push / class reminders stay app-only. A **suspended** member sees the calm notice here as well, and the query is not sent.

- This week's classes, grouped by day (Today first). Each row: **title · time · instructor · "N spots left"**. The week window rolls with the day, so an app left open past midnight still opens on today.
- **Inline book** _(v1.8, slice 1):_ each upcoming, bookable row has a compact **Book** control — booking is **one tap from the list** (plus the "looks full — book anyway?" confirm when over capacity). ### S5 · Class detail + book/cancel

**On the web too** _(v3.2, ADR 73):_ `/class/<id>`, reached by tapping any row on the schedule or on My classes. Same reads (`class.get` + `class.attendees`), same capacity label, same soft-capacity confirm, same who's-in privacy rule. The action sits under the details rather than in a sticky bar, because a browser page scrolls to it. A cancelled class says booking is closed and a class that already started says so, so the screen is never a dead end with no action and no reason.

- Title, discipline, day/time, duration, instructor, capacity ("8 / 12").
- **You can book up to a week ahead** _(v3.9, ADR 79)._ Classes exist six months out so the owner can plan and print a poster, but a booking further ahead than seven days is noise: the gym's plans change first. Both schedules only ever show the rolling seven-day week, so this changes nothing a student can see — it closes the one way past it, a direct link to `/class/<id>` for a class further out, which now answers "You can book up to 7 days ahead". _(Amended v4.2, ADR 95: both clients now ask the same `canBook` rule the server does, so that page shows the reason and offers no Book button, instead of offering one the server then refuses.)_
- **Booking requires approval and valid branch access.** Primary action, in a **sticky bottom bar** (thumb zone; v1.8 slice 6) while the details scroll above:
  - Not booked → **Book** (success **toast**, e.g. "Booked — Tue · 18:00"). Pending members can browse but cannot book (SP1).
  - When the class is at/over capacity → the count shows **red** ("**22/20 · may be busy**") and Book asks **"This class looks full — book anyway?"** → user decides; the owner manages the room.

  - _Booking here is optional — the S4 schedule row books inline (v1.8). The shared `useBookClass` rule drives both surfaces._
- **Who's in the class:** an **approved member** sees **every booked member by name**, alphabetically, **pending ones included** (ADR [55](issues/55-attendee-names-include-pending.md) — a pending member trains on the same mat). **Drop-ins show only as a count** (e.g. "+3 others"), as does anyone with no name on file. A **pending viewer** sees only counts, no names, plus a line saying names show after approval. The viewer is never listed to themselves, so a class only they booked reads "Just you so far." _(Owner/coach see everyone by name for attendance.)_ _(Built: `class.attendees` + the pure `visibleAttendees`, v2.0 N.5.)_
- **The week's theme, if the gym shows it** _(v5.8, ADR 110)_. One line naming the curriculum block covering that class's week, on the web page and in the app. It appears only when the owner has turned it on for students (**off** by default, O13), and a student never sees the block's notes. A week the gym has not planned shows nothing, so this is never an empty label.
- Cancelled class → shown read-only as **Cancelled**.

### S6 · My classes _(its own tab — v1.2 B.2; renamed **Training → My classes** in v1.8 slice 2)_

**On the web** _(v2.8 for identity, ADR 67; v3.2 for the rest, ADR 73):_ `/profile` holds the same identity edit (name and phone; email read-only), the academy with its **address** and **Open in maps** link, **My rank** with the promotion timeline, and a link across to dues. ### S8 · Check in (QR walk-in) _(redefined in v1.8 slice 4 — booking is the pre-commit; the owner marks attendance after class. In-app scanner added in v3.0, ADR 70.)_

- **No Check-in list** (removed v1.8). The only check-in path is the **gym QR walk-in** for a student who did **not** book.
- **The member path is the Scan tab** _(v3.0, ADR 70)_. Tap **Scan** → the phone's own scanner sheet opens → point it at the gym poster → the app **books + marks attended** for the class happening now → _"You're checked in ✓"_. Camera permission is asked on the first tap, not at launch; refused for good → the tab says so and offers system settings. A phone that cannot present the OS scanner (iOS 15, Android without Play Services) is told to use its camera app on the poster. Any other code (a wifi QR, the gym's own `/join` poster) **closes the scanner** and says what to scan instead — the sheet covers the screen, so a message left behind it is a message nobody reads.
- **The poster is unchanged:** it encodes the public **`https://…/checkin/<academyId>`** page, so a first-time, no-app visitor still lands on the **drop-in form** (§4), and **"Open the app to check in"** stays there as the deep-link fallback (`matops://checkin/<academyId>`) for anyone who scans with the camera app. The scanner accepts either form and refuses anything else, including the gym's `/join/<token>` invite poster.
- **The web has no scanner** _(v3.2, ADR 73)_. It does not need one: the poster's own URL is a web page, so a student who scans it with their phone's camera app lands on `/checkin/<academyId>` and checks in there. Only scanning from **inside** the app is app-only.
- Works **even if the class is full** (you're physically there); **already booked → attend, no double-book**; **pending member → read-only, check-in refused** (SP1). Edge messages, never a dead end: not signed in → sign in then complete; no class live → _"No class to check into right now."_; not a member → clear message (owner can still mark present, O5). _(v4.2, ADR 96: the server now enforces "the class happening now" too. It had been guarding the check-in mutation with the 14-day attendance backfill window instead, so the rule described here ran on the client only. Same rule, same message, now true on both sides.)_

### S9 · Pay (dues + requisites) _(v1.3 — manual bank transfer; ADR 40)_

**On the web too** _(v3.2, ADR 73):_ `/payments` serves the owner screen (O7) to an owner and this one to everyone else, so a student who lands on the URL sees their own dues rather than a load error. Same amount, status, bank details, copyable reference and **I paid** claim. A **pending** member has no read on `payments.myDues`, so the tab is hidden and the page says dues appear after approval.

- **Approved members only.** The requisites card carries the gym's bank details, so a **pending** joiner does not see this section at all (it appears once the owner approves them). ADR 60.
- **What you owe, at a glance:** your plan, amount, and next-due date, with a status — **up to date** / **due soon** / **overdue** (derived from the due date, ADR 40; the green state means the next payment isn't due yet, which is why it doesn't say "paid" — a fresh member has paid nothing). Empty until the gym sets up your dues. _(When **overdue**, a global header (S11) also promotes it across the tab screens.)_
- **How to pay → requisites card:** the gym's bank details plus a **reference code** to put in the transfer note (copy buttons on each). You pay in your own bank app. _(No card on file, no in-app card payment in v1.3 — manual bank transfer only.)_
- **"I paid":** after you transfer, tap **I paid** (optionally attach a screenshot as proof). This raises a claim the owner confirms (O7); when they confirm, the status turns to paid and the next-due date rolls forward. _(ADR 82: while the claim is open, booking is never locked — the pending claim counts as paid for the gate.)_
- One open claim per period (you can't double-claim); the reference code is what lets the owner match your transfer fast.
- **Choose your membership** _(ADR 82)_: an approved student with **no plan yet** picks one here (list of the gym's active plans, price per month, one confirm). First payment lands on the **1st of next month** — the join month costs nothing in-app. One-shot: after that only the gym changes the plan (O7). If the gym has exactly **one** active plan, approval assigns it automatically and this step never appears.

### S13 · Notifications _(v3.7, ADR 77 — web + phone)_

- **A feed of everything the gym has sent you**, newest first, one per academy. A cancelled class, a changed time, a payment reminder, a welcome, and the gym's own news all land here, whether or not the push arrived. This is the durable copy; push is the alert.

Same in the Expo app and on the web's mobile top bar. The tab bar stays on the four jobs (§S8), and the web's desktop sidebar keeps its Notifications item with the unread count. ## 3. Owner / instructor web app (Next.js)

**Owner onboarding is seamless too (A3).** Same low-friction sign-in (social or email + magic link, minimal fields). Schedule setup has quick tools so building a week isn't a chore: **duplicate a template**, **copy a day**, and a "typical week" starter. The owner should reach a working schedule + printable QR in minutes.

**Signing in lands you in your academy (ADR 49, amended by ADR 128).** Creating a gym is rare, so it is **not** pushed at everyone on login — it's a separate **"Start a gym"** link on the sign-in page (→ `/start`), and "Start a gym" makes you the active owner at once, no waiting. It gives you a gym and no branch (FP1), so it lands you on `/overview` to create the first one. On a normal sign-in the web routes you by what you belong to: **one** academy → it opens automatically; **several**, or **none but you own the gym** → the **overview** at `/overview`; **none at all** (a brand-new member) → **"Join your gym"** — type the academy's 6-digit code, add name + phone, and land **pending** as a student (same gate as an invite link).

### O0 · One gym, more than one mat _(v7.2, ADR 124)_

- **A gym is an organization; each of its academies is a place of its own.** One login runs both. Each academy has its own name, time zone, address, schedule, roster, dues, curriculum, six-digit join code and public booking link, and shares nothing with its sibling but the owner.
- **The URL names the academy.** Every screen lives under `/entities/<id>`, so two browser tabs can show two mats at once, a reload keeps the one you were on, and a link you paste to a coach opens the right place. The app stores the academy the student picked and names it on every request the same way.
- **A person can be different things at different mats** — an owner at one and a student at the other — and switching is a link, not a setting. Approving, suspending, archiving and removing someone happens at one academy and leaves the other untouched.
- **`/overview` is the way out of a mat that does not mean choosing another.** An owner sees the organization's readings once at the top, then **one card per academy** carrying that mat's own enrolments, visits, places taken, classes this week and the work waiting on it, mats with work first. No academy is listed twice. Someone who is not an owner sees the list of academies they may open and nothing more. A person who trains at two mats counts once in the totals, and two currencies are never added together.
- **Add academy** is owner-only and starts empty: its own schedule, roster, dues and join code, with nothing copied from the first. _(v7.6, ADR 128: the FIRST branch is created the same way, from the overview of a gym that has none. Creating the gym no longer creates it. See FP1 to FP4.)_
- **Not in this release:** any place that is not a BJJ academy, moving a member or a class between academies, a shared roster, and billing of any kind.

### O1 · Dashboard _(the "run the week" home)_

- **Needs you**: an action queue of blockers, each linking to the screen that clears it. Every staff member sees **attendance review** for past classes (see below). Owners also see **payments to confirm** (pending "I paid" claims), **pending approvals**, **overdue members** (count, plus what they owe in total when a single currency is in play — two currencies are never added together), and two lead items _(v6.2, ADR 115)_: **new drop-in leads** (nobody has replied yet) and **trained, not joined** (they turned up at least once and are still not a member). Both reach zero, because a lead now carries a **stage**, the handled flag it was missing. That is why the old 7-day window on the leads count is gone. They open O4 on the matching filter (`?tab=leads&stage=new` / `&stage=trained`). Other items appear only while their count is above zero. Attendance keeps its explicit zero state when the queue is clear. The overdue card opens O4 with the **Overdue** filter already on (`/members?tab=members&filter=overdue`).
- **Attendance review** _(staff, ADR 133)_: the dashboard counts every ended, live, non-cancelled class in the current branch that needs review. There is no look-back limit. Empty and partly marked classes remain visible. The summary opens Attendance with **Needs review** selected. At zero, keep **All past classes reviewed** visible. A reviewed class returns to the queue when its roster or attendance changes.
- **The day block:** the day's classes with booking counts and the coach's name — the caller's own are marked **You**. Each row carries the class's state: **Now** while it is running, **Needs review** once it has ended without a current attendance review, and a quieter title once it is done. **Clicking a row opens the class roster** — the same panel as O5 (mark present/absent, mark all present, add a walk-in), so attendance is taken without leaving the dashboard. Once **today's last class has ended** the block rolls forward and shows **Tomorrow** instead: a finished day is a log, not a plan, and anything still needing a mark is already in the queue above. A rest day with classes tomorrow rolls the same way.
- **Quick counts:** active members, classes this week. "Classes this week" counts from today forward, even though the read reaches back a week for the unmarked hunt.
- **Insights** (v1.2 C.1, trimmed in D.4, money added in D.5): a handful of numbers, each carrying the baseline that makes it mean something. **Confirmed this month** — owner-confirmed payment money for the academy calendar month, against last month, **owner-only** (instructors never see money). "Confirmed" is the only honest basis: dues are manual bank transfers (ADR 40), so money exists once the owner has matched a transfer to a claim, never before. Currencies are never added together — one figure per currency, each with its own previous month, and a currency billed in either month appears in both so a drop to zero is visible. A gym that has confirmed nothing sees no money figure rather than a zero. **Visits this week**, against **the same point** in last week — a part-week measured against a whole one reported a collapse every day until Saturday, so Wednesday noon is compared with last Wednesday noon. **New members this month**, against last month (both sides count only retained members, so the two are biased the same way and stay comparable) — owner-only. **Attendance rate** over 30 days, shown only when the gym marks absences, otherwise it would read a meaningless 100%; it carries no comparison, because a 30-day window has nothing to compare itself against. Below them, the **busiest classes** over the same 30 days, each drawn as a **bar of its share** of the top class and filled with its **discipline colour** (cool gi / warm no-gi / neutral open mat, ADR 53) — the length is the data, the hue says what kind of training. A comparison figure is coloured by direction (up `--success`, down `--warning`, never `--error`); the values themselves stay neutral, because the product does not know what a good one looks like. _"Visits this month" was dropped: a part-month count says less than the weekly pulse, and its own baseline (last month) falls outside the 30-day read this block is built on._ Each number states its own period, so the block no longer claims one window for all of them. From attendance already stored (ADR 39).
- **Who's drifting** (v1.2 C.1, owner-only): members who used to train here but have gone quiet — banded `cooling` / `at risk` / `dormant` by days since their last session — each linking to their member detail (O4) so the owner can reach out.
- **Layout** _(v4.9, ADR 101)_: the "needs you" queue is one **instrument panel** across the top, in every state — filled, all clear, and first run all fill the same strip, so the cleared queue holds its slot instead of drawing a different box. Below it, on a wide screen, the day block and "who's drifting" take the main column and **Insights sits in a right rail**, so the gym's numbers land on the first screen instead of below the fold. On a phone the same blocks stack. The screen title is **Dashboard**, with today's date under it.
- _(No standalone quick-action row — v4.9, ADR 101. Take attendance is the first queue item whenever a register is waiting, Add a class is offered by the empty day block, and both screens are in the sidebar. Approving members moved into the "needs you" queue earlier, where it only shows when someone is waiting.)_
- **First run** _(v3.6, ADR 76)_: an owner whose academy has no active members and no classes this week sees a **three-step checklist** where the queue would be, not "All clear." An empty gym has nothing to do, and the empty state reads as finished rather than not-started. The three steps link to where they happen: add your weekly classes, share your join link, print the QR poster. It disappears on its own once either count moves. Nobody is standing next to a new owner to say this out loud.
- **Pending approvals** are shown to **instructors** too _(v3.6, ADR 76)_, since they can clear that queue now (O4). The rest of the owner queue (payments, coach requests, overdue, leads) stays owner-only.

### O2 · Schedule (classes)

- **Week calendar** of concrete classes, each showing **N/Capacity** booked. On a wide screen this is a **timetable grid** (ADR 53): Monday to Saturday across, hours down, each class placed at its real time, with the coach's name on the block. A colored left rail splits the board at a glance (gi teal, no-gi amber, open mat dashed and railless), a violet line marks the current time, and runs of empty hours collapse into one "no classes" band. On a phone the same week stays a day-by-day list.
- **Click a class** (grid block or phone card) to open its **detail modal**: who is on it and the same attendance controls as O5 — mark present/absent, mark all present, add walk-ins. It is the O5 roster panel, reused. A class further out than tomorrow, or older than the 14-day backfill window, shows a read-only attendee list (the server refuses to mark there).
- Per-class actions: **cancel** (sends booked students a **push + email** — see §4), edit capacity/instructor, add a **one-off class**.
- Jump to **O3 templates** for the recurring engine.
- New/edited schedules **appear immediately** (classes are materialized on save; a daily job only extends the calendar forward).
- Empty state: "Create your first class."

### O3 · Recurring templates

- List of templates; **create/edit**: title, discipline, **days of week**, start time, duration, capacity, instructor, status (active/paused).
- **Time zone is not asked for** _(v2.9, ADR 68):_ a new template takes the **academy time zone** (O6); an existing one keeps the zone it was created with, so changing the academy setting never cancels a class someone already booked. The form shows the zone in force, and offers a one-click move when it disagrees with the academy's.
- Explains materialization plainly ("runs every Mon/Wed at 6:00 PM"). Pausing stops future generation; existing classes stay. Deleting soft-deletes (existing bookings preserved).
- **Classes are generated six months ahead** _(v3.9, ADR 79)._ Saving a template fills that window at once, so a new or edited template shows up immediately and the calendar does not run out mid-term. Nothing runs on a schedule: the horizon is the owner's to fill.
- **Rebuild schedule** _(v3.9, ADR 79, owner only)._ Pausing or deleting a template stops new classes but leaves the ones it already made on the calendar. One button at the foot of the Templates tab throws the forward schedule away and regenerates it from the **active** templates alone, six months out. Before the owner confirms, it names the damage from a live count: "Removes 34 upcoming classes and 12 bookings." **Students lose those bookings and are not told.** What it never touches: **past classes and all attendance history** (a past class carries its attendance on the same row as its booking), **one-off classes** the owner added by hand (nothing would recreate them), and a class the owner **deliberately cancelled** at a time the templates still want (it stays cancelled). Per-instance edits are discarded, because "rebuild" means the templates win.
- **Export poster** _(ADR 54):_ one button turns the active templates into a landscape PNG the owner can post or print — the weekly board with times, class names, and coaches, and **no booking numbers**. Paused templates are left out. A preview shows first; **Download PNG** saves it. The footer carries the gym's **6-digit join code** and an "Updated <date>" stamp, so a member can tell whether the board is current. No dates on the board itself, so it stays true until the schedule changes. The button sits on **This week**, beside **Add class** — printing the board is something an owner does while looking at the week, not while editing the engine behind it. It still draws the **templates**, not the week on screen, so the board stays true past the seven days shown.

### O4 · Members (people + leads)

- **Four tabs**: **Requests** (pending approvals, default, with a count badge when someone waits), **Members** (the active roster), **Drop-in leads**, and **Archived** _(v4.1, ADR 84, owner-only, last on purpose)_. Approvals block a person from training, so they come first. Every tab is **addressable** (`?tab=requests` / `?tab=members` / `?tab=leads` / `?tab=archived`), so each O1 queue item opens the tab that clears it; arriving from the "who's drifting" widget (`?member=`) opens on **Members**, so the deep link still lands on the person. The query string survives a reload.
- **Pending approvals** tab: approve / reject **students**. Someone invited as an **instructor** is approved on the **Coaches** screen instead (O10), so the two queues match the two decisions. Approving assigns the intended role and activates them. A pending person's bookings simply become normal bookings on approval.
- **Who can open this screen** _(v3.6, ADR 76)_: **owner and instructor**. In most gyms the head coach runs the desk, so an instructor can open the roster, **approve and decline** students, **set rank**, read and write staff notes, and see the pending count on their own dashboard. An instructor does **not** see the dues figures (the member's plan, price and payment status are blank for them), and does not get the invite links, the drop-in leads tab, the Archived tab, the CSV export, suspend, restore, archive, or delete permanently. Those stay with the owner. **A pending coach is the owner's decision**, approve and decline both. Approving an instructor grants staff access, so the server reserves that decision for the people who run the branch.
- **Search** _(v3.2)_: the **Members** tab has a search box above the roster. Typing filters the list by **name, email or phone** as you type, and it stacks with the **Overdue** filter. It runs on the list already loaded in the browser, so there is no extra request and no page reload. An empty result says so and suggests trying part of a name.
- **Export CSV** _(v3.4)_: the **Members** tab has an **Export CSV** button next to the Overdue chip. It downloads the roster as a spreadsheet file: name, email, phone, role, status, joined date, visit count, plan, dues status and next due date. It exports **what is on screen**, so the search box and the Overdue filter narrow the file the same way they narrow the list. The file is built in the browser from the list already loaded, so there is no extra request and nothing new leaves the server. A member's name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is written with an apostrophe in front, so a spreadsheet reads it as text instead of running it as a formula. Owner-only, like the member list itself.
- **Two ways a member leaves, and they are not the same thing** _(v4.1, ADR 84)_. Both are **owner-only**.
  - **Archive** is the everyday one, and it is reversible. The member loses access at once, their **upcoming bookings are cancelled** and those seats go back to the class, and the **dues clock stops** so no reminder ever chases them again. Nothing is deleted: attendance, rank, promotion history, coach notes, payment history and referral credit all stay on the same membership row. They drop off the **Members** tab and appear under **Archived**, where one click **restores** them to active with everything intact. Cancelled seats do **not** come back, because those classes may since have filled or passed. A student who rejoins on their own with the gym code lands back on the **same** row, so their history returns whether the owner restores them or not. Archiving can never leave the academy without an owner (§4), and only an **active or suspended** member can be archived: a pending applicant is **declined** on the Requests tab, since archiving one and restoring them would activate them without ever being approved.
  - **Delete permanently** is the rare one, and it cannot be undone. It lives behind the row's **⋯ menu**, never as a plain button, and the dialog spells out what is destroyed and makes the owner **type the member's name** before the button unlocks. It erases every row this gym holds about the person: attendance, dues, payment claims, rank, promotions, notes, notifications, bookings and referral records. Students that person referred keep their record but **lose the credit**, recorded as a cleared attribution in the referral audit. Their **sign-in account is deleted too, unless they belong to another gym on matops**. An owner may wipe what their own gym holds, never another gym's records. If they ever come back they start from nothing. This is how an owner answers a **data-deletion request**; the privacy page already tells members to ask their academy first.
- **Archived** tab _(v4.1, ADR 84)_: the people who left, with their name, contact, role and joined date, plus **Restore** and the same ⋯ **Delete permanently**. It is deliberately thin, because the tab exists to bring someone back or to erase them, not to browse. **Declined applicants are not here**: they were never members, so they stay out of both the roster and this list.
- **Active members** list → **member detail** (v1.2 C.2): a tabbed panel — **Overview** (role, contact, attendance stats, drift band), **Attendance** (the member's full present-session history, paginated), **Rank** (v1.4 G.2), and **Notes** (v1.4 G.4) — plus archive/suspend — and, on a suspended **or archived** member, a **Restore** action (`membership.restore`) that makes them active again (neither was ever a dead end). Opening a member from the O1 "who's drifting" widget deep-links straight to their detail. _(Activity/audit tab deferred — needs the audit log.)_ _(Making/stepping-down **owners** lives in the **Staff** section of **Coaches** — ADR 50, moved there by ADR 63; the member list no longer has a "Make owner" button.)_
  - **Role:** Overview shows the current role as read-only. Instructors join through the instructor invite link and owner approval.
  - **Rank (v1.4 G.2, ADR 41):** the coach sets/changes the member's rank **per discipline** (e.g. _BJJ · Blue · 2 stripes_, _Judo · Green_), picking from the **seeded** rank systems (BJJ belts + stripes, Judo belts). Belt + stripes are validated against the system. **Rank only goes up** — a belt or stripe can be **bumped, never lowered**; to fix a mistake the coach **clears** the rank (removes it, keeps the history) and sets it fresh. Changing rank records a **promotion history** (from → to · date · who) shown below. **No automation, no progress bar** — the coach decides. Disciplines with no belt ladder (boxing, MMA) have no rank control.
  - **Notes (v1.4 G.4, ADR 41):** **staff-private** coaching notes on the member (injuries, goals, readiness). Visible to **all staff at this academy**, timestamped + authored; **never** shown to the member.
- **Drop-in leads** section: the **venue check-in QR poster** (print it for the gym door) sits at the top of the tab, then the **lead pipeline** _(v6.2, ADR 115)_.
- **A lead is one unit with two axes** _(v6.2)_. **Stage** is where the owner is with this person and is stored: `new` → `contacted` → `invited` → `joined`, with `ghosted` as a side exit from any point. **Activity** is what the person did and is always derived: books Wed 18:30, trained 2 times, last 12 Aug. Keeping them apart is the point: someone who trained twice and was never followed up is stage `new` with activity "trained 2 times", and that is the most valuable row on the screen.
  - `new` is automatic on the first booking or visit. `contacted` is one tap and stamps the date. `invited` is also one tap, **separate from copying the join link**: an owner often copies a link to look at it, or sends it through a channel matops never sees, so a copy is not evidence that anybody was asked. `joined` is automatic on a **phone match at self-join** (matched on the last nine digits, so a country code written on one form and not the other still matches; two leads ending in the same nine digits match nothing), or set by hand as a **link to a member**, never a free-text flag. `ghosted` is the owner's own decision and **deletes nothing**: one tap puts them back to `contacted`.
  - **Make them a member** _(v6.3, ADR 118)_: the owner turns a lead into a member without the person doing anything first. The dialog shows the **email** back before writing, because that email is the account they will sign into: **matops sends nothing**, so the owner tells them themselves. A lead who never agreed to be contacted (the front desk asks nobody) does not offer the action at all; a lead with no email is offered it, and the dialog asks for one. A **new** address becomes an **active** member at once, since the owner just decided. An address that **already has an account** lands **pending** on the requests tab instead, because that is a real person, possibly at another gym, who never asked to join this one. Their name and phone are left alone. Students only; a coach seat stays O10's job.
  - **Filter chips** by stage, `new` first, `ghosted` behind its own chip. The count badge sits on **New** only _(v6.3)_: a number on every chip reads as data rather than as a queue. **Four numbers on top**: leads, booked, trained, joined. That funnel is what proves the channel.
  - Each lead records **where it came from**: the shared link, the gym's website, the wall QR, the front desk, or the API.
- **A visitor's classes are their own rows** _(v6.2, ADR 115)_. Before this a lead held a single class pointer, so a visitor recorded on Monday and again on Wednesday vanished from Monday's register. Now each class a visitor books or attends is its own row, with the same booking and attendance axes a member's row has. Drop-ins still count in **no** member statistic, attendance rate or coach workload.
- **Academy ID:** a plain **6-digit number** shown to copy + share. Members enter it — in the mobile app (S2, docs/issues/32) or on the **web** sign-in gate ("Join your gym", ADR 49) — to find and join the gym. Joining by ID lands **pending/view-only** as a student, same as an invite link.
- **Invite links:** live on the screen that uses them (ADR 63) — the **student** link on **Members**, above the tabs, under "How students join"; the **instructor** link on **Coaches**, in the **Staff** section. Either way the joiner lands **pending/view-only** until the owner approves — so a forwarded link never grants access on its own. One active link per role. Opening a link while **still pending** replaces the intended role, so the **newest link wins** (a student who then opens the instructor link is approved as an instructor). Opening one while **already active** changes nothing. An instructor must join through the instructor link.
- **Show QR** _(v2.2):_ every invite link (student **and** instructor) can be shown as a **QR code**, because the moment a student joins is almost always face to face at the gym. The owner holds up their phone, the joiner scans it with the phone camera and lands on the join form — no typing, no copy-paste, no spelling an email out loud. The same card prints, so a gym can put the student join code on the wall next to the check-in poster.
- **Send someone a link** _(v6.8, ADR 122)_: a **Share** control in the shell — the sidebar on desktop, the header on mobile — open to **all staff** on **every screen**. It holds the three links a person asking about training needs, side by side, each saying who it is for: **Book a trial** (`/book/<code>`, for someone who has never trained here), **Drop in at the gym** (`/checkin/<id>`, the wall poster as a link, for someone already on the mat) and **Join the academy** (`/join/<token>`, for someone who has decided to sign up). Each row copies the link, opens the phone's share sheet, or shows a QR. The links are **copied**, not moved: Settings keeps the public booking setup, and Members keeps the invite card and the printable check-in poster, which is where an owner configures them. It also names the two failures a bare URL hides — **public booking is off** (an owner can switch it on from the row; an instructor is told to ask them) and **no class is open for booking in the next week**, checked against the same window the visitor's page asks for, so the link is never sent to an empty page. An instructor may **read** both links; **minting, rotating and revoking** stay owner-only, as does the publishable key.
- **Venue check-in QR:** one static poster per gym (it covers both app check-in and the drop-in form), viewable and printable from the **Drop-in leads** tab — the tab it fills.
- **Hosted visitor booking** _(ADR 135 supersedes ADR 115's integration API)_, in Settings, **off by default**. Switching it on opens the shared booking link at `/book/<code>` and its printable QR. The hosted page owns class listing, booking, private return status, and cancellation. There is no publishable API key or allowed-origin setting.
- **Nothing is public until a class says so** _(v6.2)_: **"Share this class publicly"** on the recurring template and on a one-off class, off by default. The flag is copied onto each class when it is generated, **and onto the future classes again on every template save**, so ticking it on a template that has already filled the horizon shares those classes too. A single date can still be unshared on the class itself without touching the template. This is also how kids classes and competition training stay off the internet.
- **What the public surface can never show** _(v6.2, the deny list)_: capacity, booking counts, how full a class is, any member or visitor name, any instructor name or id, template ids, plans, prices, dues, notes, ranks, attendance, anything about who trains at the gym, and any class the owner has not shared. It returns the gym's name, zone, address and map link, and per class the id, title, discipline, start time with its offset, duration and whether it can still be booked. An unshared class answers "not found", never "forbidden", so the API cannot be used to confirm a private class exists.
- **A public booking always makes a lead** _(v6.2)_, never an account and never a member. Capacity never blocks it. Booking closes **60 minutes** before the class starts: that stops a stranger booking a class already on the mat, and someone at the door is a walk-in the gym QR handles. The visitor gets a cancel link once; cancelling takes them off the register and **keeps the lead**. The picker **groups the classes by day** and opens on the **next 3 days that have one**, with "Today" / "Tomorrow" named as such and the rest of the week behind a **Show the rest of the week** link, because a flat list of every class in seven days is a wall a stranger has to read before they can pick. Each class is **one line** (time, title, length), and **picking one collapses the whole list** to that class with a **Change** button, so the form is on screen the moment there is something to fill in rather than a scroll below it. Days and times are drawn in the **gym's** zone, so a visitor reading from another country sees the gym's today, not their own. The page must show the consent line with the privacy link beside it, and say the waiver is signed at the desk. Integrator reference: [public-api.md](public-api.md).

### O5 · Attendance

- **Review list (ADR 133):** show every ended, live, non-cancelled class in the current branch, oldest first. Default to **Needs review**, with **All** as the other status filter. Optional start and end dates use the branch time zone. Results have 50 rows per page. Each row shows the date, class, coach, review status and unmarked count. The branch picker changes the branch being reviewed.
- Open a class to see booked members, self check-ins and visitors. Existing classes start **Not reviewed**, even when every person already has an attendance mark.
- **Finish attendance:** staff must resolve every unmarked member and visitor on the register before finishing. Cancelled bookings do not block completion. If nobody is present, staff must explicitly confirm **Nobody attended**. This also clears a class with an empty register.
- **Review changes:** a later roster or attendance change makes the class need review again. Finishing from an older roster fails with a message to reload. The completed view records who reviewed it and when.
- **Historical corrections:** permission to edit attendance older than 14 days is pending a product decision. Listing past classes does not itself widen that permission.
- Mark **present / absent** with one segmented control per row. A filled **green** dot means present, a filled **red** dot means absent, **two empty rings** mean nobody has marked that person yet. The header counts carry the same three dots, so the tally and the rows read as one thing. Clicking the mark that is already on clears it back to unmarked. Self check-ins arrive already marked present — the owner just confirms.
- **Add someone who isn't listed** — one place, two cases:
  - **A member who didn't book:** search the academy's active members (anyone not already on this class), tick them, mark them present. The search matters once a gym passes a screenful of members.
  - **A visitor who isn't a member:** first name and phone (last name optional). This writes the **same drop-in lead** the public QR form writes, attached to this class, so the person appears on the roster and in the owner's leads for follow-up. Deduped by phone: a returning visitor reuses their lead and gains a row for the new class, and the register of every earlier class keeps them _(v6.2, ADR 115)_. Staff **cannot** give contact consent on someone else's behalf, so a staff-recorded visitor starts with **no consent** — and recording them never clears a consent, an email or a name they gave earlier themselves.
  - **A visitor can be marked present or absent** _(v6.2, ADR 115)_, like anyone else on the register. Before this a visitor was present by construction, so someone who booked online and never arrived could not be recorded as a no-show. A cancelled visitor booking leaves the register entirely.
- **Mark all present** _(v1.3, F.2):_ one tap marks everyone who booked as present; the owner then removes the few no-shows — faster than adding one by one. Respects the 14-day backfill window.
- _(This is the payoff of QR check-in: most attendance is already filled in before the owner touches it.)_

### O6 · Settings

- **Academy name** — the owner can **rename** it: a mutable label, _not_ unique-constrained. Identity is the **Academy code** — a unique, permanent 6-digit number shown read-only (students type it to join; it never changes) — plus the internal org id. **Owner-only.**
- **Country** _(v6.5, ADR 119)_: which country code every phone field on every screen starts on, picked from a list. Until an owner sets one it is guessed from the time zone, and the setting shows the guess and says it is one. A zone is not a country: zones cross borders, and matops maps the common ones rather than all of them.
- **Time zone** _(v2.9, ADR 68):_ the academy runs on one IANA zone, picked from a list so a typo cannot be stored. Every class time, week boundary, and statistic is drawn in it. A **new** class template inherits it; templates that already exist keep the zone they were created with, because re-timing them would cancel classes students have booked. Settings names any template zone that disagrees, and the template form offers a one-click move. _(Disciplines toggle: later.)_
- **Address** _(v2.9, ADR 68):_ free text, up to 200 characters, with an **Open in Maps** link. Students see it on the mobile Profile tab and on the join confirm step, so nobody joins the wrong gym after mistyping a digit.
- **Map link** _(v3.1, ADR 71):_ an **optional** share link from Google Maps, Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap, or Waze, pasted next to the address. When it is set, the student's tap opens that exact pin; when it is not, the tap searches the address text as before. It exists for the gyms an address string cannot describe — a unit in a mall, a basement, a building with no number. Only those four hosts are accepted, because the value is rendered as a tappable link for every member. The field is disabled until an address is typed: students tap the **address text**, so a pin with nothing to read shows up nowhere. While editing, the card shows a **Check the pin ↗** link that opens exactly what the student's tap will open — the only honest way to validate an address without a geocoder.
- **Delete the academy** _(v2.9, ADR 68):_ owner-only, and the owner must type **`delete <academy name>`** to confirm — the phrase names the gym so it cannot be done to the wrong one by habit. It is a **soft** delete: every member loses the academy at once (each lands back on the post-login gate) and its 6-digit code is never reissued to another gym, but the data survives, so a mistaken delete is reversible in the database. No restore screen, no export, no hard purge in v1. It closes **one academy**, not the gym _(v7.2, ADR 124)_: an owner running two mats can shut one, and the other keeps its classes, roster, dues, invite links and join code. The organization closes with its **last** academy, since an owner group with no place to run classes is a closed gym.
- **Booking rule** _(ADR 82)_: the **"Members need an active membership to book"** switch (off by default) and the **grace period** in days (0-30, default 7). It is an academy-wide rule, so it sits with the academy, not on the Payments screen. Full behavior in §4. **Owner-only.**
- **Everything else moved out** _(ADR 63):_ each control now sits on the screen that uses it — **Staff** + the **instructor invite link** on **Coaches** (O10), the **student invite link** and the **venue check-in QR poster** on **Members** (O4), the **bank details** on **Payments** (O7). The rules did not change, only where they are.

### O7 · Payments _(v1.3 — manual bank transfer; ADR 40)_

- **Four tabs:** the screen splits into **To confirm** (pending "I paid" claims, with a count badge), **Member dues** (each member's plan + next-due date, grouped by **Overdue / Due soon / Paid / No plan set**), **Plans** (the academy's membership plans), and **Bank details** (the gym's bank details, free text, which feed the student's requisites card S9 — ADR 40, moved here from Settings by ADR 63). Same data and actions as before — one job per tab instead of one long page.
- **Edit a plan:** a plan's name, price, and currency can be changed after it is created (`payments.updatePlan`). Members on that plan show the new name and price immediately (their dues read the plan live); payment claims already confirmed keep the amount they were confirmed at. Deleting stays a soft-delete.
- **Membership plan + dues:** the owner sets a plan (name, price, currency, cycle) and each member's next-due date. The date defaults to the **1st of next month** — billing is anchored to the start of the month for everyone, so the day a member joins never changes when their dues fall due. The owner can still pick any date. No card processor, no auto-charge — this is manual reconciliation. **Editing the date never changes the plan:** the form holds the member's actual plan id, and a member sitting on an **archived** plan keeps it (shown as "archived" in the picker) so a date-only edit cannot silently move them onto a different price (ADR 59).
- **Who paid / who's overdue:** the O4 member list shows a **status dot** per member — **up to date** / **due soon** / **overdue** (derived from the due date, ADR 40) — with an **Overdue** filter. Member detail shows next-due + last confirmed payment.
- **Confirm claims:** a "claims to confirm" list of student **"I paid"** submissions (S9). One tap to **confirm** (rolls the member's next-due date forward by the cycle) or **reject**. The student's reference code matches the bank-transfer note.
- **Booking rule** _(ADR 82)_: set on **Settings** (O6), not here. When a student is past the window, the O4 member row shows a **"booking locked"** tag beside the dues dot.
- **Auto-assign on approval** _(ADR 82)_: approving a **student** in an academy with exactly **one** active plan creates their dues row automatically, due the **1st of next month**. With several plans the student picks their own on S9; the owner can always assign or change from Member dues.
- **Payment reminders:** push + email, **one per member per billing period** — sent when the member is within 3 days of the due date, or once they are overdue. A member who stays overdue is not messaged again until the next period. Sent by a **daily** sweep (`/api/cron/remind`), scheduled from this repo's GitHub Actions rather than by the host (ADR [75](issues/75-daily-cron-on-github-actions.md)). This is the only automatic message a student gets without a person acting.

### O8 · Install as an app (PWA) _(v1.5 — owner on the mat; ADR 42)_

- The owner/instructor web app is an **installable PWA**: add it to the iOS / Android home screen and it opens **standalone** (no browser chrome), with the matops icon + brand splash. No app store, no separate binary — the same web app earns its keep on the phone.
- It exists for the owner's **on-the-mat, one-handed, five-second** jobs: take attendance, cancel / edit a class, **share** the invite link + venue QR through the phone's native share sheet, and approve a pending join — the same screens, tuned for thumb reach at phone size.
- **Cut on mobile (desk-first / the deferred-native tail):** the O1 dashboard analytics reflow, editing payments / plans / settings, and **native push + offline** — dues + class notices ride the existing email / reminder path (§4). Design intent, not a port of the desktop layout.
- **Students install it too** _(v2.2, ADR 57):_ the manifest scope is the whole site, so the student week at `/` has always been installable — nothing told a student so. The student screen now carries a quiet **"Keep this on your home screen"** card at the bottom: an **Install** button where the browser offers one (Android / Chromium), and the **Share → Add to Home Screen** wording on iOS, where no browser prompt exists. It disappears once the app is running standalone, and it is **not** shown to a suspended member. It names installing as the best experience rather than pointing at an app store, because **there is no store listing yet** — the iOS build is blocked on an Apple developer account and Android has never been submitted. A dead "Download the app" button would cost more trust than it buys. When a listing exists, this card is where the link goes.

### O9 · Referrals _(internal attribution; ADR 52, layout ADR 56, owner-only ADR 69)_

- **Owner-only, referrer-first (ADR 56, narrowed by ADR 69):** the screen lists the members who brought students in, ranked by how many, each with their students and the owner-confirmed revenue those students paid this academy-calendar month and lifetime. Students nobody is credited for sit in a second **"Nobody credited yet"** section. Membership state (pending / active / suspended; removed hidden by default) shows on each person as a dot and a word. It exposes **no contact details, plan, dues, or payment-confirmation controls** — those are O4 and O7.
- **One permanent initial assignment, recorded in one dialog:** **"Record a referral"** asks two questions — which student, and who brought them in — and says above the save button that only an owner can change it later. Clicking a student with nobody credited opens the same dialog with that student filled in. Any retained member of this academy may be the referrer — student, instructor, owner, suspended, or removed. A student cannot refer themselves and has at most one current referrer.
- **Owner correction + audit:** the owner can replace or clear an existing attribution — they click the student's chip on a referrer's card and get the same dialog, with **"Remove the credit"** as an option and the audit history behind a link. Clearing leaves a locked, auditable unassigned state; only the owner correction flow may assign it again. Every assignment, correction, and clear records old/new referrer, actor, and time. Corrections move all historical reporting to the current referrer; the old attribution remains only in audit history.
- **Reporting:** one sentence at the top says how many members brought how many students in, and what those students have paid this month and in total. Two controls only — a search that finds a person by name (matching a referrer or any of their students) and **show removed students**, which the counts and totals follow. Revenue includes confirmed claims recorded before attribution and stays grouped by currency — incompatible currencies are never added together.
- **Tracking only:** no commission, referral discount, balance, payout, referral code/link, export, or student-visible referral UI in this release.

### O10 · Coaches _(workload and coverage; v2.5, ADR 62)_

- **Who taught it is now recorded on purpose.** Every recurring template and one-off class has a **coach picker**, and every class roster (the O5 attendance screen and the O2 class detail) has a **change the coach** control for the night somebody covers. Before v2.5 the field silently held whoever created the template, so the figures below only describe classes created or corrected since.
- **Any staff member may set the coach on a class that has not started. Only an owner may change one that already happened**, because that rewrites reported history. Correcting a finished class **notifies nobody** — a student cannot act on a class that is over.
- **Credit follows the class.** A class counts for whoever its coach is right now, with no separate confirmation step. So reassigning a past class moves that class's figures to the new coach, the same way an O9 referral correction moves reporting. The screen says so.
- **What a card shows,** over a window of this month, last month, or the last 90 days: **classes led**, **time on the mat**, **students taught**, **average headcount**, and **how much of it got marked**. Cancellations show when there are any. Expanding a card gives which classes, by weekday, by week, and the list of classes led with each one's headcount or a "not marked" tag; that last list shows the **8 most recent** and says how many more the window holds _(v5.0, ADR 102 — on a 90-day window it ran to dozens of rows and pushed every coach below it off the screen)_.
- **Changing the window keeps the figures on screen** _(v5.0, ADR 102)._ The numbers dim while the new window loads and are replaced in place, instead of the page collapsing to placeholders and springing back. Expanding a card animates open and closed rather than snapping.
- **Averages cover marked classes only,** and the card says in words when some are unmarked. A gym that forgets the register would otherwise read a missing mark as a weak coach. Classes led and time on the mat are always true, so the card leads with them.
- **Never a leaderboard.** Cards are ordered by name and are never sorted by headcount: a 07:00 class fills less than a 19:00 one whoever teaches it, so ranking coaches that way measures the timeslot. **An owner sees every coach; an instructor sees only their own card.**
- **Coach requests land here, not in O4.** Someone who opens the **instructor** invite link waits at the top of this screen (owner-only), with **Approve** / **Decline**: approving grants the schedule and attendance tools, which is a staffing call. The Members requests tab stays the **student** queue, and the O1 queue carries the two counts separately ("Pending approvals" → Members, "Coach requests" → Coaches).
- **The staff list lives at the bottom of this screen** _(ADR 50, moved by ADR 63):_ every active owner and instructor, the **instructor invite link** to add one, and the quiet per-person **⋯ menu** to make a co-owner or step an owner down (the last active owner can't be stepped down, §4). Owner-only — an instructor sees only their own card and no staff list.
- **A coach who leaves keeps their row,** marked "no longer staff", so the per-coach numbers still add up to the gym's.
- **Not in this release:** pay of any kind (hourly rate, amount owed, payroll periods), more than one coach per class, per-coach retention, export, and any student-visible view of this.

### O11 · Profile _(the person, not the gym; v2.8, ADR 67)_

- **Settings is the academy; Profile is you.** Every signed-in person on the web reaches **Profile** from the sidebar footer — their avatar, name and role, which is where a web app puts the account. On a phone the same avatar sits in the header. It is a route with **no nav item**, like O5 Attendance.
- **Your details:** name and phone are editable, email is read-only because it is the sign-in identity. Same fields, same rules and the same `account.updateProfile` procedure the mobile Profile (S7) has always used, so a coach who edits their name on the phone sees it on the web and the other way round.
- **In this academy:** the academy the URL names, the caller's role there (or "pending approval"), and the month they joined. Read-only — nobody changes their own role here. A person at two mats sees each one's answer on that mat's Profile, which is why the screen lives under the academy rather than above it.
- **Appearance and sign out** live here too, next to the account they belong to. Both stay in the **desktop sidebar** as well: on a wide screen the theme toggle and sign out are one click from anywhere and are not worth a detour. _(v7.1, ADR 123: they are **not** in the phone header. It was carrying seven controls at 32px each, under the size a thumb can hit, and these two were its only duplicates. The avatar beside them opens this page, so both stay two taps away.)_
- **Something broken?** _(v3.6, ADR 76)_ A feedback link sits in the sidebar footer and on Profile. It opens an email with the academy, the person's role and the screen they were on already filled in, so a report arrives with enough to act on. The pilot runs at gyms nobody from matops is standing in, and a coach who hits a bug and shrugs is a bug we never hear about.
- **Not in this release:** avatar upload, email change, password or passkey management, per-academy notification preferences, and any student-facing bio.

### O12 · Announcements _(gym news; v3.7, ADR 77)_

- **An owner or an instructor writes an update and picks who gets it:** everyone, students only, or coaches only. The button names the number it will reach ("Post to 34"), taken from the same rule that picks the recipients, so nobody posts blind.
- **It lands in every recipient's notification center and pushes to their phone.** _(v3.8: nothing the gym sends goes out by email. matops has no mail layouts worth sending, so the feed plus push is the whole delivery.)_
- **Only active members receive one.** A pending person has not been approved yet; a suspended or removed one has lost access while keeping their history.
- **The sender gets their own copy**, which is the proof it went out.
- **Every post keeps the name of the person who sent it.** The feed row shows it under the message, so a member knows who wrote it and the gym has a record. The name is stored as it read at the time of sending, so a later rename or a coach leaving does not rewrite history. System messages (class changes, dues reminders) carry no name.
- **The compose box sits on `/notifications`,** above the feed it writes into. Owners and instructors both get it; students do not.
- **Not in this release:** editing or deleting a post, scheduling one for later, drafts, attachments, and any "who read it" report.

### O13 · Curriculum _(the plan that runs your week; v5.2, ADR 104, reshaped v5.3, ADR 105, the timeline v5.4, ADR 106, categories v5.5, ADR 107, the Monday week v5.6, ADR 108, the running block editable v5.7, ADR 109, the theme on a class v5.8, ADR 110, many curriculums v5.9, ADR 111, several blocks on one stretch v6.0, ADR 112, a note on one run v6.1, ADR 113)_

- **Schedule → Curriculum**, a third tab beside This week and Templates. Two halves: your **blocks** on the right, and **the plan** on the left.
- **A gym can run more than one curriculum** _(v5.9, ADR 111)_. A **track** is one plan with its own run of weeks: Fundamentals, Gi, No-gi, Advanced. A row of chips above the timeline switches between them, and you edit one at a time. Every gym starts with one track called **All classes**, so a gym that teaches one thing never sees a choice it did not ask for.
- **Your blocks are shared by every track.** The library is the gym's, not the track's. Closed guard can run in Fundamentals in March and in Advanced in June, and it is one block both times. A card says which tracks it is on when it is on more than one, and a block placed in any track still cannot be deleted from the library.
- **Each track moves on its own.** Lengthening a block in Fundamentals moves the blocks after it in Fundamentals and nothing else. Settled, running and draft are decided inside a track.
- **A class follows a track through its recurring class** _(v5.9, ADR 111)_. One **Curriculum** picker on the class template, next to Discipline, appears once the gym has a second track. Every class that template generates follows it. A one-off class, or a class whose template nobody assigned, follows the default track. Changing a template's track changes what its classes read at once and rewrites no class.
- **The default track can be renamed but not deleted**, because every unassigned class reads it. **Deleting any other track** _(v6.0, ADR 112)_ takes its timeline with it, leaves every block in the library, and sends the classes that followed it back to the default track, which is what an unassigned class already reads. The confirm says how many classes that is. **Rename and Delete live in a menu on the track's own chip**, behind a three-dot button that only the track you are looking at shows, so the chip row stays a row of names.
- **Blocks are filed under categories** _(v5.5, ADR 107)_. Each group in the library shows its name and how many blocks are in it, and **starts closed**, so the rail is a short list you open one part of rather than every block at once. An instructor sets the category while writing the block, from a dropdown of the ones the gym already uses that ends with **New category…**, and typing a name that already exists in another spelling files it under the existing one rather than starting a second group. A block does not have to have a category, and a library where nothing does shows a plain list with no headers.
- **A stretch of weeks can teach more than one block** _(v6.0, ADR 112)_. Standing and Pinning can run over the same two weeks. Drop a block on top of a stretch that is already on the plan and the two run together; drop it on the line between two stretches and it starts a stretch of its own. A stretch holds between 1 and 10 blocks, and refuses the same block twice.
- **The blocks on one stretch share its weeks.** They start and end together, and the length control, the arrows and the remove button belong to the stretch, not to any one block on it. To give a block its own run of weeks, put it on its own stretch.
- **The blocks on a stretch are drawn as columns** side by side, so the card reads as "these run at the same time". They wrap onto another row when there are more than the width fits, and each column carries its own edit button. A block leaves a stretch through the small remove button in its column, and that changes no dates. The last block leaves by taking the whole stretch off the plan.
- **A block is a theme your gym teaches**, with notes and a usual length in whole weeks. It is yours to keep and to use again: closed guard can be on the plan in September and again in February, and it is one block both times. The card says how many times it is on the plan.
- **The plan is a run of weeks that is always there**, even with nothing on it: a week grid running down the page with a hairline at each week, week numbers and month labels on the rail beside it, and the same violet now-line the week grid draws marking today. It shows the whole plan plus six blank weeks, and at least sixteen weeks in all, so there is always somewhere to aim.
- **A stretch's height is how long it runs.** A two-week stretch is exactly twice a one-week stretch.
- **The week starts on Monday** _(v5.6, ADR 108)_. Every row on the timeline is a Monday-to-Sunday week, matching the week numbers on the rail and the week grid on the This week tab. Every block starts on a Monday and ends on a Sunday: the first block you place opens on the Monday of the week you are in, and because blocks run whole weeks one after another, every block after it lands on a Monday too.
- **Drag a block from the library onto the timeline**, and the plan opens under the cursor to show where it will land. **Drag a placed block's grip** to move it: everything between shuffles live. **Drag a placed block's bottom edge** to make it longer or shorter, a week at a time, and every block after it slides as you pull. Nothing is saved until you let go.
- **A block on the plan shows its notes**, so the column is the lesson plan rather than a label. A one-week stretch leaves them out, because there is no room for them.
- **Each run of a block takes a note of its own** _(v6.1, ADR 113)_. The block's notes say what it covers and are the same everywhere it appears. **This run** says what these weeks are doing about it, with this group, and belongs to that placement alone: writing one changes no other run and does not touch the block. It sits under the block's notes on the plan, marked down its left edge, and is labelled "This run" on the class.
- **A run note can still be written after the weeks have passed.** It is the one thing a settled stretch accepts besides renaming its block, because a note records rather than schedules. That is where "what we actually covered" goes until the taught line exists.
- **A run note is staff-only**, like the block's notes, whatever the student switch says. Taking a block off the plan takes its run note with it.
- **Every block carries an edit button**, on the plan and in the library, and both open the same block, so a typo is fixed wherever it is read. A block that already ran keeps its edit button and nothing else: its name can be corrected, its dates cannot.
- **The same jobs are on buttons**: Add to plan, the − and + stepper, the up and down arrows and the remove button. The resize handle also takes the arrow keys, so the whole screen works without a pointer.
- **The plan is a sequence, not a set of dates.** Blocks run one after another with no gap. Drop one in, take one out, or change how long one runs, and every block after it moves with it.
- **Three states, two of them editable** _(v5.7, ADR 109)_. A block that **ended** is settled: a record, flat, muted and washed, with an edit button for its name and nothing else. The one covering **today** is running: raised, with a dot, and **the same controls as a draft** - grip, the − and + stepper, both arrows, edit and remove - because "we are not doing three weeks of this after all" is the same correction as "we need another week". One that has **not started** is a draft, and looks and behaves the same minus the brand edge.
- **The past never moves.** A block that has **ended** cannot be resized, moved or taken off, and nothing can be dropped in ahead of one. That is the only frozen state: the block running now can be shortened, lengthened, reordered and taken off like any other, because it has not finished happening. Its dates are the plan's, not the register's - a class that was taught keeps its own row on the schedule whatever the plan says later.
- **The week is the unit.** A block runs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 weeks, with 4 labelled "a month". Nothing is ever stored as "a month". The length on the plan can differ from the block's usual length without changing the block.
- **Every change to the plan can be undone and says what it did.** One line names the block, how many blocks after it moved, and the plan's new end date, with **Undo**. That covers dropping one in, moving it, changing its length and taking it off.
- **Renaming a block renames it everywhere it appears on the plan**, past blocks included, because a block is a label rather than a record of what happened.
- **A block that is on the plan cannot be deleted** from the library. It has to come off the timeline first, and the message says how many times it is used.
- **The theme shows up on the class** _(v5.8, ADR 110)_. Every block covering a class's week is listed at the top of the class, above the roster, so a coach reads the plan on the screen they already open to take the register. That is the Schedule modal, the dashboard's day panel and Attendance at once, plus the class page on the web and in the app. Staff see the block's notes under the theme. A class in a week no block covers shows nothing.
- **Students see the theme only if the owner turns it on** _(v5.8, ADR 110)_. One checkbox on this screen, owner-only, **off** until they set it. Instructors always see the theme, on or off. **Students never see the block notes or the run note**, whatever the setting: both are the coach's lesson plan.
- **Any staff writes** (owner or instructor), tracks included. Students do not see the plan screen itself, whatever the setting.
- **Not in this release:** the taught line **per class** (a run note is per stretch of weeks, so a class still says what is **planned** for the week rather than what that class covered), any structure inside the notes, soft dates for blocks far out ("early October"), repeating a run of blocks forward, copying one track into another, ordering or colouring the chips, ordering the blocks inside a stretch, dragging a block from one stretch to another (take it off and drop it again), and showing students one track but not another (the O13 student checkbox is the whole gym's).

**On a phone the bottom bar holds five slots** _(v7.1, ADR 123)_: past that the tail moves into a **More** menu and the badges of the hidden items are summed onto that tab, so a waiting payment claim still shows. An owner has seven destinations, which at 390px gave each label 53px and truncated all of them; a student has four and never sees More.

**Owner web includes O1–O13.** **O11 Profile and O12's feed are for every role, students included** (the O12 compose box is staff only). Instructors see O1–O3, **O13**, **O4** (the desk: approve, decline, rank and notes, but no dues figures, invites, leads, CSV, Archived tab, suspend, archive, delete or role picker — v3.6, ADR 76), O5 and **O10** (their own card only; no O6 Settings, O7 Payments controls, or O9 Referrals) — the shell hides those nav items and the server procedure tiers guard them. _(v3.3, ADR 74:_ typing the URL of a page your role does not have no longer renders it. The shell says the page is not part of your account and offers the way home, rather than filling the screen with refused queries. The tiers are still the guard; this only decides what a person is offered.) O8 describes installability rather than a separate data screen. _(ADR 38 role split, extended by ADR 52, narrowed by ADR 69.)_

## 4. Cross-cutting rules

- **Uniform approval (students + coaches):** everyone joins via a link → **pending / view-only** (browse schedule, book, check in — no names, no tools) → owner approves → the intended role is granted (student features, or instructor schedule+attendance tools). A forwarded link never grants access by itself.
- **Capacity is a soft guide, not a gate:** booking is **never blocked**. A class shows "booked / capacity"; at/over capacity it turns **red** ("22/20 · may be busy") and asks the user to confirm. The owner manages the room. (Hard caps, priority, waitlists, no-shows, cancel cut-offs → next iteration.)
- **The membership booking gate** _(opt-in per academy; ADR 82)_: with **"Members need an active membership to book"** switched on (O6 Settings, off by default), an **approved student** may book only while their membership is in order. One derived clock (`bookingAccess`, pure, in `@matops/domain`) with a **grace window** (`graceDays`, owner-set 0–30, default 7): **no plan** → grace runs from the later of joining and the moment enforcement was switched on (so flipping the switch never locks anyone instantly); **on a plan** → booking stays open through the due day plus the grace window. Past the window, `booking.book` refuses with the exact strip message. **An open "I paid" claim unblocks immediately** — the student is never punished for the owner's confirmation latency; a reject re-locks by derivation. Never gated: **pending** members (provisional bookings), **staff**, **cancel**, and the **QR check-in** (the person is at the gym; the owner handles money face to face). Enforcement off → nothing anywhere changes.
- **Privacy on the schedule:** only **approved** members see who's in a class (names) — a pending viewer sees counts alone, so a self-join never turns into a member directory. An approved viewer then sees **all booked members** by name, **pending ones included** (amended by ADR [55](issues/55-attendee-names-include-pending.md); it was approved-only). **Drop-ins stay a count** — they consented to the gym contacting them, not to being listed to students. Owner/coach see names for attendance.
- **Check-in (QR):** one static **gym QR**. App users → book+attend in a tap; marks present even if the class is "full". No-app **first-time visitors** → the drop-in form (below).
- **Drop-in / lead form (public web, first visit):** the same gym QR opens a short **public page** (no login): first name, second name, phone, **email** _(required since v6.4, ADR 118: it is the address the owner turns into a member account, and a lead without one can only be reached through a channel matops never sees)_, a **consent checkbox** ("OK for the gym to contact me"), and **which class they are here for** _(v6.3, ADR 117)_. Saved as a **lead + attendance**, no capacity impact. The owner follows up with an app link. QR-inside-the-gym is the anti-spam.
- **Which class the visitor came for** _(v6.3, ADR 117)_: the form **asks** rather than guessing. It offers the classes **on now** (picking one marks them **present**) and the ones **coming up** (picking one **books** them, so staff can mark them present or absent later), plus **"I'm just visiting"**, which keeps the lead with no class. One class on and it is pre-selected, so the common case is still one tap. Before this the server attached a class only when exactly one was running, so two classes at once left the lead attached to nothing and a visitor who scanned between classes could not say what they wanted to try. The pick is re-checked on the server against a freshly resolved list: a class that ended while the form sat open, one the owner cancelled, and one belonging to another gym are all refused.
- **Policies open where the person is** _(v6.7, ADR 121)_: on both public forms the privacy link and the training risk notice open in a **dialog**, never at their own URL. The form stays mounted, so a name, phone, email and class pick already typed are exactly as they were left. `/privacy` and the dialog render the same text, so they cannot disagree.
- **A second tick, for the risk** _(v6.7, ADR 121)_: a required checkbox, "I understand training is full contact and carries a risk of injury", with the notice behind it. The notice names the **academy**, because the liability limit is the gym's, and it says plainly that it is **not the waiver** — that is still signed at the desk. The tick is enforced in the browser and **stored nowhere** — no column, no migration, and that is settled rather than deferred: the gym already relies on the **waiver signed at the desk**, and a tick on a stranger's phone would be a weaker copy of a document that exists on paper. A visitor booking through an integrator's own page does not see the notice, because the REST contract is unchanged and the desk waiver covers them the same way.
- **A form never goes dead** _(v6.6, ADR 120)_: on every public form, the submit button is disabled **only while the request is in flight**, never because a field is invalid. Pressing it either sends or says what is wrong, on the field it belongs to, in words the person can act on. The phone box refuses characters a number cannot contain as they are typed, so what is on screen and what the form holds cannot disagree. A message clears as soon as that field is edited, and a refused drop-in refetches the class list so a class that closed while the form sat open stops being offered.
- **Privacy page** _(v3.6, ADR 76)_: a public `/privacy` page, in plain English, names who is responsible for the data, says the academy controls its members' data while matops processes it, lists what is stored and why, and says how to ask for a copy or a deletion. It says the data is stored in the **EU** and that whoever handles it does so on our instructions, but **does not name our vendors** _(v6.7, ADR 121)_: a visitor deciding whether to leave a phone number does not need our stack, and a vendor list goes stale the first time one is swapped. Every form that collects a stranger's details links to it: the drop-in consent line and the join form. The link sits **outside** the consent checkbox's label, so tapping it never ticks the box.
- **Rate limits on the public surface** _(v3.6, ADR 76)_: the join QR goes on a wall, so the pages behind it have a brake. A drop-in form can be submitted 5 times per 10 minutes from one address, an academy or invite lookup 30 times a minute, and a magic-link sign-in email 5 times per 5 minutes. Over the limit the page says to wait a minute and try again. Sign-in email is the one that matters: it is a shared quota, so one flooder would break sign-in for every academy at once.
- **Notifications (v1 — push + the notification center, no full comms domain):** a thin helper sends. _(v3.8: no gym message is emailed. Email is sign-in only, until matops has mail layouts worth sending.)_
  - **Class cancelled** → **push** to every booked student.
  - **Class reminder** → push, ~2h before. On mobile this is a **local notification the phone schedules when the student books** and drops when they cancel (ADR [54](issues/54-local-class-reminders.md)), so it needs no scheduler. The server-side sweep behind `/api/cron/remind` stays built but **switched off** — ADR [75](issues/75-daily-cron-on-github-actions.md) schedules that route daily for dues only, and a daily tick would reach only the classes inside the 2h window. **A web-only student gets no class reminder**; the S11 overdue strip is what chases them.
  - **Membership dues** → push, one per billing period, due-soon or overdue (O7 above, ADR 75).
  - **Membership approved** → push ("You're in at {Academy}").
  - **Staff announcements** _(v3.7, O12)_ → the notification center + push.
  - **Every one of the above also lands in the notification center** _(v3.7, S13; ADR [77](issues/77-notification-center.md))_, written before the send, so a failed push never costs the member the message.
  - Delivered via Expo Push (mobile); Resend carries the sign-in magic link only. _No live in-app updates (WebSockets) in v1_ — screens stay fresh by light polling; the unread badge polls with the rest of the shell.
- **Time zones:** every academy has one; classes render in it. No per-user tz in v1.
- **Empty / error states** are first-class (crowded-niche UX bar): every list has a purposeful empty state; every mutation has an optimistic path + a human error toast.
- **Loading states** _(v3.9, ADR [80](issues/80-loading-states.md))_ are the third of the three, and the rule is that the placeholder looks like what follows it. On the **web**, a route draws the skeleton of its own screen from the moment you click it until the data lands, so nothing jumps into place. On the **phone** each screen draws its own shape the same way, from the week of classes to the dues card. Where there is no screen to imitate yet - the cold open, the academy gate, the magic link, an invite opening - the **basketweave mark weaves itself** instead, with a line saying what is happening ("Opening your academy"). A wait shorter than 180ms shows nothing at all. _(v4.7, ADR [91](issues/91-cache-components-and-the-static-shell.md): the two printed-QR landings and "Start a gym" now paint their page frame from the cache first and weave the mark where the form will be, so the 180ms rule no longer applies to the frame itself, only to what fills it. Typing the sign-in address while already signed in shows that page for a moment before it forwards you.)_ _(v4.8, ADR [100](issues/100-the-client-router-keeps-a-page-for-30-seconds.md): on the web, **going back to a screen you opened in the last 30 seconds draws no skeleton at all** - it comes back as you left it, with no wait. Longer than that and it loads normally. The first time you open a screen is unchanged.)_ A spinner is left with one job: an action already in flight, like a button mid-submit. Reduced motion holds every placeholder still.
- **Offline:** not supported in v1 (documented non-goal); the app assumes connectivity, degrades to a clear "you're offline" state.
- **Freshness (mobile):** the student tab screens (Schedule / My classes / Profile) **pull to refresh**, and the app refetches stale reads when it comes back to the foreground. A student who reopens the app never acts on a spots-left count from hours ago.

### Edge rules (v1 defaults — the small cases, settled)

- **Editing a class:** change **time or instructor** → booked students are **notified** (push + the feed), **unless the class already happened** — correcting who taught a finished class is bookkeeping, and a student cannot act on it (ADR 62). **Cutting capacity** below current bookings is allowed (soft cap — just shows red).
- **Editing a template:** applies to **future, non-cancelled** instances only; past and cancelled classes are untouched. A **timing** change (days / start time / zone) **cancels** the future occurrences rather than deleting them, then re-materializes: an occurrence the new timing still covers comes back **scheduled with its bookings intact**, and one the new timing drops stays visible as a **cancelled** class. A booked student never loses a class silently (ADR 60).
- **Deleting a class / template:** soft-delete; existing bookings/attendance are preserved; a deleted (cancelled) class notifies its booked students.
- **No gym message goes out by email** _(v3.8)_: the notification center plus push is the whole delivery. matops has no mail layouts, and a plain-text notice from an unknown sender is how a domain gets marked as spam. Email carries the sign-in magic link and nothing else.
- **A pending person keeps attending:** the member list shows each pending member's **visit count** ("attended 3×") so the owner is nudged to approve them. No auto-approve.
- **Duplicate drop-ins:** leads are **grouped by phone** — one lead per person with a **visit count**, not a row per visit.
- **Sole owner:** an owner can **promote another member to owner** (co-owner) from the member list, so a gym is never left ownerless. Full ownership transfer / account deletion is deferred.
- **The owner of the gym is an owner at every academy of it** _(v7.3, ADR 125)_: the person who runs the organization reaches all its mats with owner rights, whatever their membership at one mat says. Otherwise a co-owner could archive them at the second mat and only a button on that mat could put it back. The same person can still be **erased from one mat** — that clears the roster there — but the erase never takes their gym away, and never deletes their account, because nothing in the app can make someone the gym's owner again.
- **The academy switcher only offers mats you may open:** a membership that was suspended, archived or removed is left out, rather than offered and then refused at the door.
- **Removing / rejecting someone with history:** their **attendance and lead records are kept** (they did train); access is revoked. Re-joining later starts pending again.
- **Referral history survives removal:** an archived or declined student is hidden from O9 by default but can be included for historical reporting; they remain eligible as referrers while their membership row exists. A **permanently deleted** member is the exception: their row is gone, so any credit pointing at them is cleared and the students they brought in move to "Nobody credited yet" (v4.1, ADR 84).
- **QR scanned with no class on:** the app shows "No class right now"; the public form still captures the person as a **general visit lead** for that day.

## 5. Explicitly NOT in v1

Mirrors the map's Out-of-scope: **card payment processing / auto-subscription / billing** (_note: manual-transfer dues ARE in v1.3 — see below_), **referral commissions / discounts / payouts / automated codes**, **belts & rank progression** (_note: member rank IS in v1.4 — see below_), **curriculum & games library**, a **full comms domain** (in-app DMs, email newsletters — _note: gym announcements + transactional push ARE in v1, §4_), **live in-app updates / WebSockets**, **cross-academy aggregated views**, **session packs**, **multi-currency / i18n**, **super-admin UI**, **rich offline/sync**.

Clarifications from the friction wave:

- **Public _booking_ page** was OUT; **reversed in v6.2** (ADR [115](issues/115-public-booking-api-and-the-lead-path.md)). A gym can now share a booking link and a public API, off by default and per class. What still stays out: payments on it, confirmation or reminder messages, webhooks, a digital waiver, guardian consent, waitlists, a visitor login, and any public write other than a booking.
- **Capacity hygiene → next iteration:** hard caps / blocking a full class, priority for registered members, **waitlists**, **no-show tracking or penalties**, and **cancellation cut-offs** are all deferred. v1 shows a soft red "may be busy" warning and lets the owner manage the room.

Scope additions (v1.3 "Frictionless & Pay"):

- **Payments → v1.3 (manual bank transfer only).** Membership dues, a **requisites card**, an owner-confirmed **"I paid"** claim, member list **paid / overdue** status, and dues **reminders** ARE in v1.3 (S9, O7, ADR 40). **Card processing / auto-subscription / Stripe stays OUT** — a candidate only after the manual flow proves demand.
- **Home widget** (today's classes + one-tap book/cancel) is scheduled as the **v1.3 fast-follow** (S10, R.2), right after the launch build — needs native widget code (ADR 27, promoted).

Scope additions (v1.4 "Rank & Notes"; ADR 41):

- **Member rank → v1.4.** Per-discipline ranks the **coach sets** (BJJ belts + stripes and Judo belts seeded; the model is general), a student-visible **promotion timeline** (O4, S7), and **staff-private notes** on a member (O4) ARE in v1.4. **Still OUT:** attendance→promotion **automation / progress prediction**, owner-defined **custom rank ladders**, **bulk** promotion, and a **student training journal** (submissions / rolls / competition log / session notes — a separate product, rejected not deferred).

Scope additions (v1.5 "Owner on the mat"; ADR 42):

- **Owner PWA → v1.5.** The owner/instructor web app becomes an **installable PWA** for on-the-mat jobs (O8). **A unified both-roles native app stays OUT** — the two clients keep their natural axis (**student = native / casual**, **owner = web / admin + now mobile**). A **separate** native owner app is revisited only on a native-only mat need (reliable push, offline attendance, a home widget) **and** the Apple-account / device-test blockers clearing. **Student web** also stays OUT (rejected — Kano _Indifferent_, no natural laptop moment; the shared router keeps it cheap to revisit).

The architecture leaves seams for the deferred items ([architecture.md](./architecture.md) §8).

## 6. Definition of done (v1)

A new academy can: owner signs up → creates a recurring schedule → prints the **gym QR** and shares the **invite link** → a student joins on their phone (magic link, minimal info), sees the schedule at once, and **books before approval** (provisional) → the owner approves → the student **checks in with one QR tap** at the gym → a **drop-in without the app** is captured via the QR web form → attendance is mostly filled in before the owner lifts a finger. Both loops work end to end, with low student friction and full owner data, on a portable, expandable foundation.
